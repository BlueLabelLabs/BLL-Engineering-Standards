The hard rules from this pack are numbered guidelines and live in the
[guidelines catalog](../guidelines/README.md). What follows is the soft half:

## Decisions this archetype introduces

- payment_processor · commerce_build · catalog_search

## Preferences

- Build commerce **custom** (composes `web-application` + `server-side`) rather than an off-the-shelf platform.
- Payments: **Stripe** for almost everything; **subscriptions always Stripe**. POS systems: **Clover POS** (deep experience) or **Square**.
- Catalog / product search: a dedicated **search service**.
- Cart / checkout / orders: custom in the app; **Stripe** handles payment and tokenization.
- Inventory & fulfillment: custom.
- Tax: **Avalara** when needed.
- Receipts are emailed (email provider per `universal-baseline` — Mailgun).

## Selection guidance

- The storefront/app follows the `web-application` pack; the backend follows `server-side`; auth, data, monitoring, and email follow `universal-baseline`.
- Keep PCI scope off your servers — let Stripe's hosted fields handle card entry; store only tokens + last-4.
- Recommended artifacts: order/payment flow (including Stripe webhooks), checkout sequence, catalog/search data-flow.
